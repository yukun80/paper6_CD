\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{CCDepth}} \\
Input and grid & New flood mask and FABDEM on the same grid. Support domain is the intersection of the mask with valid DEM. \\
Boundary statistics & Pair radius 2 pixels. Peer radius 3 pixels. Slope scale $5^\circ$. Dispersion scale 1 m. MAD scale 1.4826. $\sigma_0=0.25$ m. Minimum 3 samples per side. \\
Anchor eligibility & High-weight threshold 0.5. Minimum 3 anchors. Minimum span 2 pixels. \\
Objective & $\lambda_C=1$. $\lambda_B=10$. $\lambda_T=1$. $\mu=\lambda_B[10^{-3},10^{-4},10^{-5},10^{-6}]$. Hard terrain lower bound $Z+0.01$ m in the interior. Soft lower bound in the boundary band. \\
Initialization & Weighted boundary-interval midpoint. Unsupported components receive status 0. \\
Solver & Four-color sweeps. Six-candidate coordinate updates. 10 sweeps per check. At most 2000 sweeps per stage. Two consecutive stable checks. \\
Convergence & Defaults: fixed-point residual 0.001 m and relative objective change $10^{-6}$. Runs reported here: fixed-point residual 0.5 m and relative objective change 0.001. \\
Output & Solved depth $\max(S-Z,0)$ on the success domain. Published depth retains $S-Z>0.01$ m. Other locations are NoData. \\
